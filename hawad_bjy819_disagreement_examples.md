# Disagreement Examples — hawad & bjy819

## Example 1 — amrex-codes/IAMR

**Author:** rising  
**Validator:** irregular  

**Reason:** Activity increased from 2018 to 2021, then dropped sharply. The author focused on the early growth, while the validator considered the full trend.

**Resolution:** Validator kept. “Irregular” better describes the rise followed by the sharp drop.

## Example 2 — mobvoi/wenet

**Author:** declining  
**Validator:** irregular  

**Reason:** The author looked at five years of activity and saw a decline. The validator only looked at the last year, which showed irregular activity.

**Resolution:** Author kept. The full five-year history better supported “declining.”
